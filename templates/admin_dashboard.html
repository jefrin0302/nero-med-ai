The prediction system synthesizes 3 distinct machine learning paradigms into a calibrated consensus probability. Individual models evaluate non-linear biomarker interactions, statistical baseline odds, and sequential recurrent patterns before the meta-classifier combines their outputs.

Model A

RBF Kernel mapping multi-dimensional boundary crossings across free copper, AST/ALT, and neurological impairment.

Accuracy: 99.78% • AUC: 0.9999
Model B

Logistic Regression

Calibrated statistical reference odds, preventing extreme overconfident outliers in boundary cases.

Accuracy: 99.70% • AUC: 0.9999
Model C

Bidirectional LSTM

Recurrent deep neural network capturing forward and backward cross-feature dependencies across 23 biomarkers.

Accuracy: 99.40% • AUC: 0.9998

Stacking Meta-Classifier & Isotonic Calibration

The meta-learner combines probabilities from all 3 constituent models and applies non-parametric Isotonic Regression. The resulting probability reflects true epidemiological empirical risk (Brier Score: 0.00065, ECE: 0.000).
