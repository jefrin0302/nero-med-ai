👁️ Kayser-Fleischer (KF) Ring Clinical Assessment Guide

Kayser-Fleischer rings represent golden-brown copper deposition in Descemet's membrane of the cornea and are present in >90% of Wilson's Disease patients with neurological involvement.

🔍 Slit-Lamp Examination Criteria

  • Location: Peripheral limbus of the cornea in Descemet's membrane.
  • Color: Golden-brown, greenish, or brownish copper ring.
  • Reversibility: KF rings fade and completely disappear during successful chelation therapy (Penicillamine/Trientine).

🧪 Diagnostic Correlation

  • Neurological Cases: KF rings are present in ~95% of patients presenting with tremor or dysarthria.
  • Hepatic Cases: KF rings are present in ~50% of hepatic presentations.
  • Leipzig Score Point: Presence of KF rings adds +2 points to the Leipzig Diagnostic Score.
