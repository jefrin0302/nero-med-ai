🧠 Clinical Chunking Mechanics: Preserving Medical Semantics
Why fixed-character window chunking risks clinical safety, and how NeuroMed AI protects dosages and page provenance.
❌ Naive Fixed-Character Chunking Clinical Hazard

Slices raw text mechanically at exactly 500 characters, ignoring punctuation, abbreviation periods, and medical numerical values.

"...recommended dose of Trientine is 20 mg/kg daily in divided doses. In pediatric patients, do not ex"
--- CHUNK SPLIT 500 CHARS ---
"ceed 1000 mg/day as higher initial doses cause severe neurological deterioration..."
⚠️ Failure Mode: A query on pediatric Trientine retrieves Chunk 1 without Chunk 2, losing the critical upper limit restriction ("1000 mg/day max").
✅ NeuroMed AI Sentence-Preserving Chunking Production Safeguard

The pure shared engine chunk_clinical_text() protects periods in dosages (e.g. mg., kg., i.e.) and preserves the complete sentence unit.

[Source: AASLD_Guideline_2022.pdf | Page: 14]
"The recommended initial dose of Trientine is 20 mg/kg daily in divided doses. In pediatric patients, do not exceed 1000 mg/day as higher initial doses cause severe neurological deterioration."
[150-char Overlap Region into Chunk 2]
🛡️ Safety Guarantee: Complete dosage guidelines, upper safety thresholds, and authentic page references remain intact in a single contextual unit.
