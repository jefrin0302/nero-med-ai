Mandatory Clinical Decision Support Notice & Disclaimer
NeuroMed AI is an assistive clinical decision-support tool, not a confirmed medical diagnosis or diagnostic device. Predictions, probability metrics, and criteria breakdowns must be evaluated in context by a qualified, licensed clinician. Definitive diagnosis of Wilson's Disease requires comprehensive specialist hepatology/neurology evaluation, complete Leipzig scoring, ophthalmic slit-lamp examination for Kayser-Fleischer rings, 24-hour urinary copper testing, and genetic/biochemical confirmation.
